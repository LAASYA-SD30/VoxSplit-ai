Speech Sensitivity 0.50
Controls how confident the model must be to call a chunk "speech". Lower = picks up quiet or distant voices. Higher = only counts loud, clear speech. Default 0.5 works for most recordings.
Min Silence Gap ms
How long a pause must be before it splits into a new segment. 300 ms is good for natural speech. Raise it (e.g. 800 ms) to merge nearby phrases into fewer, longer segments.

How It Works
01

Decode & Normalise

torchaudio reads your file in any format and converts it to 16 kHz mono — the exact input the model needs. WebM/Opus from the recorder is converted via ffmpeg before processing.

02

Neural Scoring

Silero VAD, a lightweight recurrent network, scores every 30 ms chunk with a probability that it contains human speech.

03

Boundary Detection

Chunks above your sensitivity threshold are merged into segments. The silence gap controls how short pauses between words are handled.

04

Preview & Export

Stream each segment in-browser or download the full silence-stripped file and individual clips as WAV.
